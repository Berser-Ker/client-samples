How It Started

One oven.
Twelve tables.

We opened in 2018 with a wood-burning oven, twelve tables, and a menu printed on a single sheet of paper. The idea was simple: cook the best ingredient you can find, as simply as possible, over fire.

We started sourcing from three local farms. Today we work with over twelve producers across the region, and the relationship with each one shapes what lands on the menu that week.

The restaurant has grown, but the kitchen hasn't changed. Same oven, same technique, same obsession with getting it right.

How We Cook

Four things we
never compromise.

These are not values for a website. They are the decisions we make every morning when the market calls.

01

Seasonal

The menu follows the market, not the other way around. If it is not in season, it is not on the plate.

02

Honest

No tricks, no foam, no unnecessary steps. The ingredient is the point. We do not cook to impress ourselves.

03

Local

We know the names of the people who grow our food. That accountability makes every dish better.

04

Simple

A perfect dish has nothing to remove. We stop adding long before we think we are done.

The Kitchen

The people
behind the fire.

A small team with a long track record. Everyone here has been cooking professionally for over a decade.

Marco Solis

Head Chef and Co-founder

Trained in Buenos Aires and Madrid. Spent six years in Michelin-starred kitchens before opening with the conviction that the best food doesn't need stars to prove it.

Valentina Cruz

Sous Chef

Runs the pasta program and the pastry section. Her handmade pappardelle is the most-requested item on the menu. Has been with the kitchen since month three.

Diego Parra

Sommelier and Co-founder

Curates the natural wine list and designs the pairings for the tasting menu. His rule: every bottle on the list is one he would drink at home on a Tuesday.
