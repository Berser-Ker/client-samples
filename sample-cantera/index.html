Our Story

Built around
the fire.

Cantera started with a wood-burning oven and a conviction: the best meals are the simplest ones, made with the best ingredients you can find. Eight years later, that hasn't changed.

We work with local producers and change the menu with the seasons. Not because it's fashionable, but because it makes the food better.

Featured Dishes

What we are
proud of.

A few dishes that define the kitchen. On the menu as long as the season allows.

From the Grill · Signature

45-day dry-aged ribeye, bone marrow butter.

Local grass-fed beef, aged in-house, cooked over oak. Finished tableside with our house bone marrow butter.

Pasta Program · Seasonal

Hand-rolled pappardelle, wild mushroom, aged pecorino.

Made fresh daily. Mushrooms from local foragers. The sauce changes with the season.

Pastry · Year-round

Dark chocolate tart, smoked caramel, sea salt.

Single-origin chocolate from a cooperative in Oaxaca. Caramel smoked over the same wood as the grill.
