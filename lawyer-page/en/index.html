History

A firm built
on trust.

Hernández Contreras, Martínez & Fitch, Abogados (HCM&F Abogados, SC), is a firm established fifty years ago by applicant lawyers, focused on legal counsel and litigation, providing professional services, both to individuals and companies, mostly in Corporate Law, Civil Law, Family Law, Commercial Law, Labor and Criminal matters, Tax Criminal Law and Administrative Law.

The firm was established by its founding partners Carlos Hernández Mendieta, Camila Graciela Contreras Blanquet and Esperanza Zepeda Contreras, specialized in Public and Private Law, working in various areas of consulting and litigation in the aforementioned matters, calling themselves then Hernández Contreras, Abogados, S.C.

Carlos Hernández Mendieta Postulant lawyer graduated from the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) in 1972, who stood out in the field of civil litigation until his death in 2004.

Camila Graciela Contreras Blanquet Postulant lawyer graduated from the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) in 1971, who excelled in the field of civil and corporate law, in the last years of her professional life she worked within the Special Affairs Division of the Legal Department of the Mexican Social Security Institute (IMSS).

Esperanza Zepeda Contreras Postulant lawyer graduated from the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) in 1967, who has excelled in the workplace, her last performance in working life was in the public service as Assistant Secretary for Technical Information and Documentation of the Federal Board of Conciliation and Arbitration.

Initially having only 6 members and a single office located in Mexico City, the firm undertook the task of advising and offering its clients the services and experience in the indicated topics.

As per the Firm expansion, it changed its name to Hernández Contreras, Martínez & Fitch, Abogados (HCM&F Abogados, S.C.), made up of four partners who are in control of the affairs, which are attended by a highly trained staff made up of 24 members, including legal and administrative personnel in their offices in Mexico City. Likewise, it has various correspondent offices through which it also attends the affairs of its clients in different States and the main cities of the Country, such as Cancun, Guadalajara, Monterrey, Morelia, Querétaro, Tijuana and Toluca.

Our history has given us the privilege of advising a large number of companies and individuals, representing prominent figures in the political, business and industrial fields, whose names are reserved out of professional ethics.
