How We Engage

From kickoff
to handover.

A predictable cadence that gives leadership clarity at every checkpoint and keeps execution honest.

01
Discovery
WEEK 1 · 2

We listen, read internal docs, sit in on operating reviews, and talk to your team. We surface the question your engagement should actually be answering.

02
Diagnosis
WEEK 3 · 4

We build a working model of where your business actually is. Not the deck version. The operating-truth version, with the constraints and trade-offs visible.

03
Design
WEEK 5 · 8

We co-design the strategy or operating system with your leadership. You own the outcome; we sharpen the thinking and stress-test the assumptions.

04
Handover
WEEK 9 · 12

We hand the system to your team and stay close for as long as it takes for it to run without us. Optional retainer for ongoing operating reviews.
