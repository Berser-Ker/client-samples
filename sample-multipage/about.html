Who We Are

A firm born
from the trenches.

Meridian was founded after its partners spent years inside growth-stage companies: building operations, fixing broken processes, and navigating funding rounds without outside help. We learned what works and what advisory firms typically miss.

In 2018, we decided to bring that experience to other companies at critical inflection points. Our first three clients came from referrals. That has not changed: over 80% of our engagements still come from people who worked with us before or were referred.

Today, Meridian works with 20 to 30 companies at any given time across North America and Latin America, spanning ecommerce, consumer goods, retail infrastructure, and B2B services.
